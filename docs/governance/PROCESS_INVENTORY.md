# Process Inventory

Each target process requires actor, trigger, state, owner, deadline, evidence, exception,
escalation, audit, and acceptance: listing-to-inquiry; showing; application/household;
screening/human decision; lease/signature/move-in; charge/payment/reconciliation; maintenance
intake/triage/recommendation/approval/assignment/verification/invoice/warranty; turns and
cleaning; contractor estimate/change order; project budget/commitment/actual; communication
capture; document/insurance expiry; delegation; migration; incident/recovery.

Current fragmented paths are discovery evidence, not target authorization. Ann validates normal
operations; Faruk validates reserved decisions; specialists validate their domains.

Contractors receive only assignment-scoped access and cannot approve their own change order,
work, or invoice. Cleaners receive a separate assignment-scoped restricted role with
time-limited entry instructions, checklists, before/after media, supply and damage reporting,
inspection, rework, invoicing, and performance history.

Ann's operations process includes authorized global search, saved views, filters, workload,
exceptions, owner approvals, and drill-down. Faruk's process is a separate material-threat and
reserved-decision queue; Nathan's platform-administration process is distinct from both.
