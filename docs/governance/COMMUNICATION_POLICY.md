# Communication Policy

Official operational communication occurs in or is captured by PerchPoint. Calls, SMS, email,
portal chat, internal notes, consent, delivery state, record links, commitments, and follow-up
remain attributable. A direct Faruk call is allowed but its material result must be recorded.

Critical events escalate immediately; decision-required items enter an authorized deadline
queue; action-required items enter assigned queues; informational items enter digest/history.
Routine work does not individually notify Faruk. Recording, transcription, retention, quiet
hours, and notices require counsel before activation.

Overdue work escalates first to the assignee, then the supervisor, requires a reason/update, and
reaches Faruk only when material or unresolved.
