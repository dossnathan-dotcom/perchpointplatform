# Payment Channel Policy

Stripe is the primary direction for ACH, card, autopay, deposits, eligible fees, and refunds.
Controlled check, cash, money order, bank transfer, and approved exceptions record payer,
recipient, amount, time, property, household, evidence, recorder, approval, deposit, matching,
and reconciliation states. Personal Cash App intake ends in the target model.

Browser success never proves money movement. Provider state and PerchPoint accounting facts
remain separate. PerchPoint supplies operational accounting; formal accounting software retains
GL, tax, close, and final statement authority.
