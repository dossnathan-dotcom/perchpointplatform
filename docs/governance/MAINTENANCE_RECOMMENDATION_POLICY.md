# Maintenance Recommendation Policy

Technicians record observed condition, probable cause, recommendation, repair/replace reasoning,
measurements, manufacturer/model/serial/compatibility, media, safety, stabilization, long-term
remedy, labor/material estimate, alternatives, consequence, recurrence risk, confidence, and
specialist need. Maintenance employees have no standing purchase authority.

A materially different selection preserves the original recommendation plus override reason,
cost comparison, compatibility confirmation, decision maker, technician review where practical,
risk acknowledgment, and resulting return/rework/delay/waste. Turns, cleaning, major/repeat/high
cost/safety/disputed work requires inspection or resident/manager verification before closure.

Operational metrics must distinguish incorrect parts, returns, duplicate trips, delays, rework,
repeat failures, temporary fixes, resident disruption, and wasted labor. These measures support
coaching and procurement decisions; they do not autonomously impose employment conclusions.
