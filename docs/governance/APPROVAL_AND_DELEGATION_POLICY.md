# Approval and Delegation Policy

Ann may approve routine allowed/budgeted work up to and including $1,200. Faruk retains total
commitments above $1,200 and all constitution-reserved decisions. Aggregate related exposure by
property, unit, project, work order, vendor, decision type, and relevant time window; splitting is
prohibited.

Delegation records grantor, recipient, authority, property/project, decision type, limit,
effective/expiry times, revocation, actions, and emergency context. No shared master code.

Provisional emergency policy: Ann may authorize up to $2,500 only for reasonable stabilization
of immediate life, safety, habitability, or property threats; immediate Faruk notice, recorded
maintenance authorization, and post-event review are mandatory. Faruk must confirm this policy.
