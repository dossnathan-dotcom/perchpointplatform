## Page 1

This recording is a discovery-style meeting between Speaker 1 and Farouk, focused on
understanding the current operational problems inside Farouk’s property management business
and identifying what kind of website, system, or automation platform would help streamline the
company.

The business appears to manage approximately 100 tenants. Farouk is currently heavily
involved in day-to-day operations, including tenant communication, payment tracking,
maintenance coordination, leasing decisions, accounting oversight, budgeting, and vendor or
contractor coordination. The main theme of the conversation is that the company has grown to a
point where its current informal systems are creating bottlenecks, manual work, missed
opportunities, and an overdependence on Farouk.

The website or platform that needs to be built should not simply be a marketing website. It
should function as an operational hub that helps centralize leasing, tenant communication,
payments, maintenance requests, document access, task routing, reporting, and internal
decision workflows.

Current Systems and Tools Mentioned

1. Innago

Farouk’s team uses Innago as a tenant-facing platform.

It currently handles:

- Tenant payments

- Tenant ledgers

- Maintenance requests

- Tenant communication

- Some admin documents

However, Innago is not fully adopted by tenants. Farouk estimates that:

- Around 60% of tenants may use Innago for payments.

- Around 40% to 50% of tenants may use it when contacting the company or submitting
maintenance requests.

This creates a problem because the business technically has a system, but tenant behavior is
fragmented. Many tenants still contact Farouk directly or use alternative payment methods.

Farouk is frustrated because Innago:

- Does not sync well with their leasing needs.

The company uses Zillow Rental for leasing and listing rental properties.

1. Leasing Is Not Streamlined

Farouk identifies leasing as likely the biggest area of opportunity cost.

The company is able to lease properties, and Farouk believes the properties are usually
presented well. However, he questions whether they are leasing fast enough and whether they
are attracting the highest-quality tenants possible.

The current leasing process relies on external platforms like Zillow Rental and Facebook. The
company does not have its own page where listings can be added, presented, and managed
directly.

The leasing process lacks:

- A centralized listings page

- A unified application funnel

- Automated lead collection

- Lead scoring or prioritization

- Better tenant screening support

- Automated follow-ups

- Vacancy tracking

- Visibility into how long each unit has been available

- Clear reporting on lead source performance

Farouk specifically points out the unseen losses:

- A unit may eventually lease, but it might have leased two weeks earlier with a better process.

- A tenant may be approved, but perhaps a better-quality or longer-term tenant could have been
found.

- These losses are hard to detect because they do not show up directly as line items.

2. Payments Are Fragmented

Even though Innago supports payments, not all tenants use it. Some tenants pay through other
methods, including Cash App.

Farouk gives an example of a tenant sending him $1,975 through Cash App. Because the
tenant paid him personally instead of through the correct business system, Farouk had to:

2. Transfer or replace the funds into the business account.

3. Notify his team so they understand why he deposited the money.

4. Manually record the payment in Innago.

5. Update the tenant ledger to reflect that the payment had been made.

This is a clear example of the operational problems created by tenant noncompliance and

- Clear tenant payment rules.

- Stronger enforcement of approved payment methods.

- Automated payment reconciliation.

- Centralized payment status visibility.

- Alerts for off-platform payments.

- A workflow for manually recorded exceptions.

5. Farouk Cannot Delegate Enough

A major theme is that Farouk is still involved in too many small decisions.

He says he often has to make spending decisions as small as $100 to $150. This prevents his

He wants to delegate more authority, but he does not yet have the systems, budget controls,

delegation.

This limits the company’s growth because Farouk’s time is consumed by small operational
issues instead of high-value activities.

Summary for Building the Website Methodically

Farouk’s business manages about 100 tenants and currently relies on several disconnected
tools, including Innago, Zillow Rental, Facebook, WhatsApp, and a new accounting service
called Short Landlord. Innago is mainly used for tenant payments, ledgers, maintenance
requests, communication, and documents, but tenant adoption is inconsistent. Zillow Rental and
Facebook are used for leasing because the business does not yet have its own rental listings
website.

The biggest operational problems are fragmented leasing, scattered tenant communication,
inconsistent payment behavior, weak real-time accounting visibility, lack of property-level budget
tracking, and overdependence on Farouk for small decisions. Farouk is constantly reacting to
issues instead of focusing on growth activities like finding deals, negotiating, and deploying
capital.

The website should be built as an operational platform, not just a marketing site. It should
include a public-facing rental website, a leasing funnel, maintenance request intake,
communication routing, daily dashboards, budget approval workflows, document reminders, and
staff accountability tools.

The first priority should be to build a rental listings website with lead capture and application
workflows. This addresses the largest opportunity cost: leasing delays and tenant quality. Next,
build a maintenance request system that routes tenant issues away from Farouk and into a
structured dashboard. Then add a daily briefing dashboard so Farouk can see urgent issues,
open maintenance, late payments, leasing status, approvals, and admin deadlines in one place.

After that, add approval workflows so Farouk can delegate small decisions while keeping control
over major spending, legal issues, budget approvals, and new lease approvals. Property-level
budget tracking should be added so the team can see whether turnovers and repairs are under

renewals, vendor agreements, and other administrative deadlines.

The MVP should include:

 1. Public rental listings

 5. Maintenance request form

 6. Internal maintenance dashboard
