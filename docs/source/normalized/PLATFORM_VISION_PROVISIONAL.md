## HawkVision Homes Centralized Property Operations Platform

Complete project vision and business value overview.

### Executive overview

HawkVision Homes can consolidate its leasing, resident service, property operations,
payments, maintenance, documents, and management reporting into one connected platform.
Prospective tenants, residents, vendors, and employees would each receive a secure
experience designed for their role, while leadership would gain a current view of activity
across the portfolio.

The platform would replace scattered handoffs among websites, inboxes, calendars,
spreadsheets, forms, and separate portals. Trusted third-party services would continue to
process specialized functions such as payments, calendar synchronization, screening,
electronic signatures, email, and text messaging. HawkVision would control the central
interface, data relationships, workflows, permissions, and reporting.

### What the platform will accomplish

- **Centralize operations.** Create one source of truth for properties, units, prospects,
  applicants, residents, leases, payments, maintenance requests, documents, vendors, and
  communications.
- **Reduce administrative work.** Automate confirmations, reminders, status updates,
  recurring tasks, document requests, payment notices, renewal alerts, and maintenance
  follow-up.
- **Improve leasing execution.** Move each inquiry through showing, application, screening,
  approval, signature, payment, and move-in without losing history between systems.
- **Improve resident service.** Give residents one secure location to pay, submit
  maintenance requests, view documents, receive updates, and communicate with management.
- **Strengthen management visibility.** Provide current information about occupancy,
  vacancies, collections, delinquency, showings, applications, lease expirations,
  maintenance activity, and vendor performance.

### Complete platform scope

- **Public leasing website:** searchable listings, property pages, media, availability, rent
  and fee information, policies, neighborhood details, inquiries, search optimization, and
  mobile access.
- **Showings and lead management:** real-time booking, calendar synchronization,
  confirmations, reminders, rescheduling, lead source tracking, staff assignments,
  follow-up tasks, notes, and a leasing pipeline.
- **Applications and screening:** secure applications, co-applicants, document uploads,
  consent, screening-provider integration, standardized staff review, status tracking, and
  decision records.
- **Leases and move-in:** lease generation, electronic signatures, deposits, move-in
  instructions, condition reports, document retention, and conversion from applicant to
  resident.
- **Resident portal:** account balance, card or ACH payments, autopay, receipts, lease
  documents, announcements, messages, insurance records, maintenance requests, renewals,
  and notice to vacate.
- **Payments and resident ledger:** rent and fee charges, credits, deposits, partial
  payments, allocations, refunds, failed-payment handling, receipts, balances,
  reconciliation support, and complete transaction history.
- **Maintenance and vendors:** resident requests, media uploads, urgency review, assignment,
  scheduling, work-order status, vendor estimates, invoices, costs, completion evidence, and
  service history.
- **Property and unit records:** central records for ownership information, units, occupants,
  leases, appliances, warranties, inspections, access instructions, documents, financial
  activity, and maintenance history.
- **Renewals and move-outs:** expiration alerts, renewal offers, updated terms, signatures,
  move-out notices, inspections, deposit disposition, turnover work, and relisting readiness.
- **Owner command center:** portfolio metrics, occupancy, vacancy, collections, outstanding
  balances, lease expirations, lead conversion, showing activity, maintenance aging, vendor
  results, and drill-down reporting.
- **Automation and AI:** operational briefings, record summaries, approved response drafts,
  property-specific assistance, natural-language reporting, workflow alerts, and
  human-approved actions with audit history.

### Connected services and data control

HawkVision employees and residents would work primarily within the HawkVision platform.
Specialized providers would operate behind the interface through secure APIs. Stripe would
process card and bank payments; calendar services would synchronize showings; screening and
signature providers would complete regulated or specialized transactions; and communication
providers would deliver email and text messages.

The platform would preserve HawkVision's operational record independently of those
providers. Payment processors would not replace the resident ledger, calendar events would
not replace showing records, and screening results would not replace documented human
decisions. This separation supports reliable reporting, provider changes, error recovery,
and a complete audit trail.

### Expected business impact

- Lower operating friction.
- Fewer missed actions.
- Potential software savings while retaining specialized processing and infrastructure.
- A scalable operating foundation.

### Project outcome

The completed project will give HawkVision Homes one branded system for leasing, payments,
resident service, maintenance, renewals, move-outs, and portfolio reporting.
