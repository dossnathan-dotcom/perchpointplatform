HawkVision Homes Centralized Property 
Operations Platform 
Complete project vision and business value overview 
Executive overview 
HawkVision Homes can consolidate its leasing, resident service, property operations, payments, maintenance, 
documents, and management reporting into one connected platform. Prospective tenants, residents, vendors, and 
employees would each receive a secure experience designed for their role, while leadership would gain a current view of 
activity across the portfolio. 
The platform would replace scattered handoffs among websites, inboxes, calendars, spreadsheets, forms, and separate 
portals. Trusted third-party services would continue to process specialized functions such as payments, calendar 
synchronization, screening, electronic signatures, email, and text messaging. HawkVision would control the central 
interface, data relationships, workflows, permissions, and reporting. 
What the platform will accomplish 
●​
Centralize operations. Create one source of truth for properties, units, prospects, applicants, residents, leases, payments, 
maintenance requests, documents, vendors, and communications. 
●​
Reduce administrative work. Automate confirmations, reminders, status updates, recurring tasks, document requests, 
payment notices, renewal alerts, and maintenance follow-up. 
●​
Improve leasing execution. Move each inquiry through showing, application, screening, approval, signature, payment, and 
move-in without losing history between systems. 
●​
Improve resident service. Give residents one secure location to pay, submit maintenance requests, view documents, 
receive updates, and communicate with management. 
●​
Strengthen management visibility. Provide current information about occupancy, vacancies, collections, delinquency, 
showings, applications, lease expirations, maintenance activity, and vendor performance.

Connected services and data control 
HawkVision employees and residents would work primarily within the HawkVision platform. Specialized providers would 
operate behind the interface through secure application programming interfaces. Stripe would process card and bank 
payments; calendar services would synchronize showings; screening and signature providers would complete regulated 
or specialized transactions; and communication providers would deliver email and text messages. 
The platform would preserve HawkVision's operational record independently of those providers. Payment processors 
would not replace the resident ledger, calendar events would not replace showing records, and screening results would 
not replace documented human decisions. This separation supports reliable reporting, provider changes, error recovery, 
and a complete audit trail. 
Expected business impact 
●​
Lower operating friction. Employees spend less time moving information between disconnected tools or searching for the 
current record. 
●​
Fewer missed actions. Central queues and automated reminders make unanswered leads, incomplete applications, 
overdue balances, aging work orders, and expiring leases visible. 
●​
Potential software savings. The platform may replace overlapping website, scheduling, form, CRM, portal, task, and 
reporting subscriptions. Specialized processing and infrastructure services would remain separate operating costs. 
●​
A scalable operating foundation. New properties, units, employees, vendors, and workflows can be added without 
recreating the underlying system for each expansion. 
Project outcome 
The completed project will give HawkVision Homes one branded system for leasing, payments, resident service, 
maintenance, renewals, move-outs, and portfolio reporting.
