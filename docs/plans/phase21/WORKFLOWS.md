# Phase 21 workflows

Ann answers on her existing phone. The desk stores metadata, notes, and follow-up tasks. It does not record audio, store a transcript, or accept a maintenance case because a call connected.

1. During the business calendar, an ordinary inbound call routes to Ann's handset.
2. Digit 9 or an emergency flag routes to the accepted on-call chain. A forwarding loop is refused.
3. Busy, no-answer, cancel, reject, and carrier voicemail stay distinct from human acceptance.
4. After hours, or when nobody answers, one callback task is opened on that interaction.
5. A separate worker may escalate an overdue callback. Escalation is not acceptance.
6. An unintegrated call is a note Ann records. A `tel:` link is not connection or duration evidence. Unknown duration stays unknown.
