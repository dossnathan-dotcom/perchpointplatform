# Accessibility

The target is WCAG 2.2 AA. Automated Axe, keyboard, focus, contrast, forced-color, reduced-motion, and reflow checks are evidence. They are not a certification.

Implemented controls include skip links, one page heading on representative views, visible focus, `forced-colors` outlines, reduced-motion handling already present in `index.css`, native buttons and links, form errors tied to fields, and table headers with sort state.

Manual NVDA with Chrome or Edge, Firefox confirmation, and VoiceOver with Safari are owner-deferred to Nathan before production. No manual screen-reader transcript is invented here.

Contrast pairs for linen on obsidian, obsidian on linen, linen on copper, and obsidian on gold are tested at 4.5:1 or better.
