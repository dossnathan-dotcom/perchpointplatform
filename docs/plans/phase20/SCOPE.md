# Phase 20 scope

Repository Phase 20 is maintenance intake, emergency triage, and a bounded
field handoff. Accepted Phase 19 remains reconciliation. The roadmap PDF still
uses different phase numbers. CONFLICT-028 records that difference.

## Moved into this phase

- Resident, staff, and worker intake on the existing `maintenance_cases` record.
- Urgency questions, a human emergency flag, and escalation that a person must accept.
- One coordinator, a proposed handoff, and an explicit accept or decline.
- Versioned material needs, a management decision, delivery, and a compatibility check.
- Time intervals and attributable corrections.
- An explicit location check-in with synthetic coordinates only.
- Before-and-after evidence and a reviewer who is not the person who submitted the work.
- Inspection notices that do not change a balance.
- Ann's queue, the technician job page, the resident report, and Faruk's briefing section.

## Still owned by later phases

- Phase 21: live telephony, IVR, transcription, and real SMS or phone delivery.
- Phase 22: full scheduling, vendors, a native maintenance app, and route optimization.
- Phase 23: a shared inbox.
- Phase 24: a generalized approval engine.
- Phase 25: budgets, procurement, and payroll.

## Closed for this phase

No vendor payment, no purchasing execution, no real GPS collection, no employee
monitoring, no public webhook, and no claim of live 24/7 coverage.
`real_values_activated` and `money_moved` stay false.
