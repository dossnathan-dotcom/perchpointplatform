# Five owner oversight areas

These lists extend the existing owner briefing. They are not a second dashboard.
A failed or stale read stays unavailable. A green zero is not used to hide that.

Clock: America/New_York. Amounts are integer USD minor units. Each row links to
its source record. Counts use distinct underlying records. One project is one
decision even when it has several lines.

## O1 — Decisions awaiting Faruk

Include pending decisions the owner is required to make. Show age, deadline,
known exposure, recommendation, and approve, deny, or request-changes.
Exclude decisions already decided and lines that belong to one open project
decision. Source: Phase 24 decision requests. Until that table exists, the
card says the source is not available.

## O2 — Work at risk

Include unacknowledged emergencies, unowned or overdue work, and breached
commitments. Show property, accountable person, severity, blocker, and next
action. One case appears once, with its reasons retained. Source: maintenance
cases and Phase 22 appointments.

## O3 — Budget and commitment exposure

Approved budget versus verified cost plus outstanding commitment. Proposed
changes stay separate. Unknown coverage stays unknown. Remaining budget is not
cash. Source: Phase 25 budgets. Until then, the card says the source is not
available.

## O4 — Money needing attention

Reuse accepted arrears, assistance, receipt exceptions, and reconciliation.
Keep resident debt, expected assistance, unmatched receipts, and disputed items
distinct. No new prediction and no financial effect. Source: the Phase 19
briefing and the October cohort. Drill-down totals must match the rows.

## O5 — Follow-through exceptions

Wrong or missing materials, repeat work, overdue callbacks, missing completion
proof, and consequential document deadlines. Group by team and type. Do not
show raw coordinates or rank workers. Callbacks begin in Phase 21. Documents
join in Phase 25.

Exports for Nathan are specified in EXPORT_DATA_DICTIONARY.md. Export permission
is not database access, a secret, unrestricted personal data, or precise location.
