# PerchPoint Product Constitution

Status: approved Phase 1 policy from the execution directive; specialist gates remain explicit.

## Purpose
PerchPoint is HawkVision Homes' complete property operating system. It centralizes routine
operator, applicant, resident, contractor, cleaner, and provider workflows while protecting
Faruk's attention for judgment, strategy, material risk, and growth.
PerchPoint is the sole target routine operational interface; approved specialized providers
remain behind its governed domain records and workflows.
Initial operations are Greater Cincinnati. Core identity, jurisdiction, organization, property,
money, and policy models must support additional cities and states without redesign.

## Authority
- Faruk Atmaca: executive sponsor and final business authority.
- Nathan Doss: technical/product authority and separately assigned authorized operator.
- Ann Springer: project/leasing manager and operational coordinator.
- Specialist authority remains with accounting, maintenance, and qualified legal reviewers.

Faruk reserves total commitments above $1,200, capital projects, final applicant decisions and
new leases until delegated, evictions, adverse actions, termination/nonrenewal, material
rent/fee/screening policy, legal matters, material write-offs, insurance claims, delegation
policy, and material production launch.

## Operating principles
1. PerchPoint replaces fragmented operator-facing systems; Innago is migrated, reconciled,
   archived read-only, and retired.
2. HawkVision's public site is the exclusive target listing/application destination. Current
   planning excludes Zillow/Facebook syndication.
3. Official communications occur in or are captured by PerchPoint.
4. Stripe is the primary payment direction; controlled exceptions remain attributable and
   reconciled. Formal accounting software retains GL, tax, close, and final statement authority.
5. Human approval remains mandatory for legal, housing, money, lease, spending, and dangerous
   maintenance decisions.
6. Production data, branding, and client-specific workflows belong to HawkVision. GitHub is
   canonical and production accounts should be HawkVision-owned.
7. Phases are evidence gates, not reduced product scope.
8. Adult residents use individual accounts linked to households; production privileged access
   requires MFA. Shared master passwords, approval codes, and implicit super-roles are prohibited.

## Phase 1 boundary
This phase preserves sources, audits Phase 0, and establishes governance. It does not implement
production auth, RLS, payments, screening, signatures, communications, imports, uploads,
operational workflows, deployment, or paid providers.
A dedicated maintenance mobile application remains a post-core program requiring separate
approval. Provider selection remains deferred and uses the approved value-for-cost framework.
