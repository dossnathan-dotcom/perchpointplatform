# System Replacement Boundary

## Replaced operator-facing surfaces
Innago, fragmented listing workspaces, WhatsApp/personal messaging as systems of record,
personal payment intake, unmanaged spreadsheets/forms, and provider dashboards for routine
work are transition sources, not the target operating model.
Innago coexistence is migration-only: migrate and reconcile its records, retain a read-only
historical archive, then retire it from routine operations.
HawkVision's public website is the exclusive target listing and application destination; Zillow,
Facebook, and other external listing workspaces are excluded from current and target policy
unless an approved change explicitly supersedes PP-PROD-003.

## Retained specialized authority
Stripe or another approved processor moves eligible funds; screening and signature providers
perform regulated/specialized transactions; Twilio/email transports communications; calendar
providers synchronize; formal accounting software owns GL, tax, close, and final statements.
PerchPoint owns domain records, UX, routing, evidence, receipts, exceptions, allocation,
reconciliation support, history, and provider abstraction.

No current Zillow/Facebook syndication, paid-provider activation, real migration, or production
integration is authorized in Phase 1.
